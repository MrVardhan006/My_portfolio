AI · GenAI · RAG
RAG Chat Application — AI Document Chatbot
What it is: an AI chatbot that lets you "talk" to your own PDF documents. Instead of relying only on what a language model already knows, it first finds the relevant parts of your document and answers from them, so replies stay accurate and tied to your file.
How it works
  1. Upload a PDF through the Streamlit interface.
  2. LangChain extracts the text and splits it into smaller chunks.
  3. When you ask a question, semantic search retrieves the most relevant chunks.
  4. Those chunks plus your question go to the Google Gemini API, which writes the answer grounded in the document.
